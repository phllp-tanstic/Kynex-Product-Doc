# FAQs

**What is Kynex?**

Kynex is a crypto-native platform that turns on-chain portfolio intelligence into prediction markets. Users can track real capital, predict measurable outcomes, and earn from accuracy.



**How is Kynex different from prediction markets like Polymarket?**

Kynex markets are tied to **verifiable portfolio and wallet data**, not abstract events. Outcomes are resolved using on-chain data rather than subjective oracles or external narratives.



**Is Kynex custodial?**

No. Kynex is non-custodial. Users retain full control of their wallets, and funds are managed by smart contracts during market participation.



**Do I need KNX to use the platform?**

Yes. KNX is required to:

* Create markets
* Participate in predictions
* Access premium intelligence
* Stake for fee sharing
* Vote in governance



**What types of predictions can I make?**

Predictions are based on:

* Wallet performance
* Portfolio ROI
* Benchmark outperformance
* Asset inflow and outflow behavior

All predictions are measurable and data-backed.



**Are there guaranteed returns?**

No. Kynex does not offer fixed yields or guaranteed returns. Rewards depend entirely on prediction accuracy.



**How are outcomes resolved?**

Outcomes are resolved automatically using:

* On-chain portfolio data
* Verified price oracles
* Deterministic resolution logic

There is no manual intervention.



**What happens if a market cannot be resolved?**

If resolution is impossible due to oracle failure or data unavailability:

* The market may be invalidated
* Stakes are refunded proportionally (minus gas where applicable)



**Can markets be manipulated?**

Kynex applies multiple safeguards, including:

* Market size caps
* Wallet eligibility filters
* Slippage controls
* Oracle redundancy

Manipulation is economically disincentivized.



**How do rewards work?**

Winners receive:

* Their original stake
* A proportional share of the losing pool
* Net of protocol fees

Rewards scale with accuracy and timing.



**What happens if my prediction is wrong?**

Your staked KNX is transferred to the winning pool participants, minus protocol fees.



**Can I stake without predicting?**

Yes. Users can stake KNX to earn a share of protocol fees without participating in individual markets.



**Is Kynex financial advice?**

No. Kynex is an information and market infrastructure protocol. Users are responsible for their own decisions.



**Are there geographic restrictions?**

Users are responsible for complying with local regulations. Certain features may be restricted in some jurisdictions.
