# Glossary

**Accuracy-Based Rewards:** A reward mechanism where users earn returns based on correct prediction outcomes rather than participation volume or activity.

**Benchmark:** A reference asset or index (e.g., ETH, BTC, or a custom index) used to measure relative portfolio or wallet performance.

**Burn:** The permanent removal of KYNEX tokens from circulation, reducing total supply and introducing deflationary pressure.

**Confidence Score:** A numerical score assigned to a wallet or portfolio based on historical performance, consistency, risk-adjusted returns, and behavioral patterns.

**Dynamic Odds:** Odds that adjust in real time based on the relative size of Yes / No pools and remaining time to market expiry.

**Fee Sharing:** The distribution of protocol-generated fees to KYNEX stakers as yield.

**Inflow / Outflow:** The net movement of capital into or out of a wallet, portfolio, or asset over a defined period.

**Kynex:** A crypto-native protocol that turns on-chain portfolio intelligence into prediction markets where users earn rewards for accurate predictions.

**KNX:** The native utility and governance token of the Kynex protocol, used for market creation, prediction participation, premium access, staking, and governance.

**Market Creation Fee:** A fee paid in KYNEX to create a prediction market, designed to reduce spam and incentivize high-quality markets.

**Market Expiry:** The fixed time when a market closes and no further stakes can be placed.

**Market Lifecycle:** The sequence of stages a prediction market follows: creation, open participation, lock at expiry, resolution, and settlement.

**Market Size Cap:** A predefined limit on the total amount of KYNEX that can be staked in a market to reduce manipulation risk.

**Non-Custodial:** A system design where users retain full control of their wallets and funds; the protocol never takes custody of user assets.

**Odds Adjustment Algorithm:** The mechanism that recalculates implied probabilities as stake flows into Yes or No pools.

**On-Chain Resolution:** The deterministic process of resolving market outcomes using blockchain data and oracle feeds without human intervention.

**Oracle:** A data provider that supplies external information (such as price data) to smart contracts for market resolution.

**Payout:** The distribution of rewards to users who correctly predicted a market outcome, executed automatically on-chain.

**Portfolio:** A collection of assets held by a wallet or group of wallets, tracked and analyzed as a single unit.

**Portfolio Intelligence:** The analysis of on-chain wallet and portfolio data to extract signals such as performance, capital flow, and behavioral patterns.

**Prediction Market:** A market where participants stake tokens on the outcome of a future, measurable event, with rewards distributed based on accuracy.

**Protocol Fee:** A percentage of funds collected during market settlement, allocated to stakers, treasury, and burn mechanisms.

**Resolution:** The process of determining a market’s outcome using predefined logic and verified on-chain data.

**Resolution Window:** A bounded time period after market expiry during which oracle data is finalized and outcomes are resolved.

**Slippage:** The change in implied odds caused by staking into a market, particularly for large positions.

**Smart Money:** Wallets or portfolios identified as having historically strong performance, efficient capital deployment, or consistent profitable behavior.

**Stake:** The amount of KYNEX committed to a prediction. Incorrect predictions transfer stake to the winning pool.

**Stake-to-Predict:** The mechanism requiring users to stake KYNEX tokens to express conviction on a market outcome.

**Staker:** A KNX holder who locks tokens to earn fee sharing and participate in governance.

**Staking:** Locking KYNEX to earn a share of protocol fees and gain governance rights, independent of individual predictions.

**Time-Lock:** A delay applied to governance-approved actions before execution, allowing users time to react to protocol changes.

**Treasury:** A protocol-controlled pool of funds used for development, audits, ecosystem growth, and long-term sustainability.

**Wallet Tracking:** The monitoring of wallet activity to understand portfolio composition, capital movement, and behavioral patterns.

**Yes / No Pools:** The two opposing outcome pools in a binary prediction market where users stake KYNEX based on their conviction.
