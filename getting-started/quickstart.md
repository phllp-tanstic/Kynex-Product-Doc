# Quickstart

This guide provides a comprehensive walk-through for on-boarding onto the Kynex protocol.<br>

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
#### **Step 1: Connect Your Wallet**

Kynex is non-custodial.

1. Visit the Kynex dapp
2. Connect a supported wallet
3. Approve the connection

Kynex never takes custody of your funds.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### **Step 2: Get KNX**

You need **KYNEX** to use the platform.

You can use KYNEX to:

* Create markets
* Participate in predictions
* Stake for fee sharing

Once KYNEX is in your wallet, you’re ready.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### **Step 3: Explore Markets**

Navigate to the Markets page to view:

* Live prediction markets
* Market expiry times
* Current odds
* Total liquidity

Each market is tied to **real, on-chain portfolio data**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### **Step 4: Choose an Outcome**

1. Select a market
2. Choose **Yes** or **No**
3. Review current odds and expiry

Your choice represents conviction, not preference.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### **Step 5: Stake KNX**



1. Enter the amount of KYNEX you want to stake
2. Review slippage and risk indicators
3. Confirm the transaction in your wallet

Your stake is locked until market resolution.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### **Step 6: Wait for Resolution**

After the market expires:

* No more stakes can be placed
* On-chain data is verified
* The outcome is resolved automatically

No manual intervention.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### **Step 7: Receive Payouts**

If your prediction is correct:

* You receive your stake back
* Plus a share of the losing pool
* Minus the protocol fee

Payouts are executed automatically on-chain.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### **Optional: Stake for Passive Yield**

If you prefer not to predict:

* Stake KNX directly
* Earn a share of protocol fees
* Participate in governance.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Important Notes:**&#x20;

* There are no guaranteed returns
* Incorrect predictions lose stake
* All markets are data-backed and transparent

Only stake what you can afford to lose.
{% endhint %}
